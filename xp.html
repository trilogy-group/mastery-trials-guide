For guides
How a raid is paidEach right answer earns 1/R XP, where R is the student's rung (1/30 XP at rung 30). A raid under 80% accuracy earns 0. Win or loss does not matter. XP is capped at 1 XP per minute spent answering.
The ladderRungs every 5 CQPM up to the grade target: K 20, Grades 1–3 30, Grade 4 35, Grade 5+ 40. A student moves up a rung when the median of their last 5 accurate raids reaches it, and never moves down.
Why a slow student can still earn 1 XP a minuteAt rung R, answering R right a minute pays R × 1/R = 1 XP a minute. The rung follows the student's own speed, so every student can earn full XP at their own pace.
Faster, but no more XPAnswering faster than the rung still pays 1 XP a minute, the cap. It moves the student up instead: at the next rung each answer is worth 1/(R+5), so full XP needs the new speed. At the top rung, full XP needs the grade target.
How long 10 XP takesOn rung 15:
Earning less than 1 XP a minuteThe student is answering slower than their rung. Rungs never go down, so a student who slows down after moving up earns less until they are back at that speed.
Earned 0 XPThe raid was under 80% accuracy. Slowing down to get more right beats guessing fast.
