How to get your FastMath test

Beat the bosses in Math Raiders. The game sends you the test.

What grade are you in?

Your boss

Titan

30 right answers in 1 minute

3 steps to your test

1

Beat the bosses in order, starting at Gloop Jr.

2

Beat Titan on every track.

3

Your first test shows up!

Play alone. Wins with friends don't count.

Grade 3 has 3 tests

They come one at a time, in this order. Pass one and the next one shows up right away.

Pass them all to move up a grade!

On every test, be…

Fast30 right in 1 minute
CarefulAlmost no mistakes

You need both to pass.

Passed?

Great job! Your next test comes right away. Pass the last one and you move up a grade.

Not yet?

No problem. Back to Math Raiders to practice that one:

1Only that test's track is open.
2Start again at Gloop Jr. and climb up.
3Beat Titan 3 times.

Then that test comes back. Pass it to keep going.

For guides
Winning, but nothing unlocksThey're playing with friends. Only solo wins count.
Beat the boss, but no test cameAnother track isn't done yet. Open each track and find the one that isn't “Mastered.”
No retest after a failThey need to climb back up and beat their boss 3 times, solo, on the failed track. A Grade 2 or 3 student usually needs about 8 rounds, around 15 minutes.
Beats the boss, but keeps failing the testWinning in the game doesn't guarantee test speed. One Grade 2 student beat Titan 3 times before every retest, then scored 21–29 on the test (30 needed), at 95–100% accuracy. Practice speed, not just boss wins.
A track is locked at the new gradeIt was already done in an earlier grade. Nothing to do there.
Failed a “test out,” nothing assignedThat's by design. Ask the FastMath lead for a manual assignment.
